E-book library for touchscreens

We love reading books

Calibre Touch is an e-book reader and standalone library manager designed for touchscreen devices. Browse, search and read your books, keep a reading log, and look after your Calibre-compatible library with a simple, touch-friendly interface.

What it does

Works with Calibre libraries

Calibre Touch can open and use existing Calibre libraries. However, this compatibility comes with absolutely no guarantee: Calibre Touch is an independent app, not affiliated with Calibre. If you use both apps on the same library, you do so at your own risk. Please keep a backup of your library.

Built for touch

Designed for every touchscreen device: phones, tablets and touchscreen laptops. Large targets, swipeable lists and a layout that adapts to any screen.

Standalone library

Works as its own e-book library manager, with no desktop Calibre installation required. Add books, edit their details and keep everything in a Calibre-compatible folder.

Built-in reader

Read EPUB, MOBI/AZW3, FB2, TXT, DOCX, ODT, RTF and PDF books inside the app, with adjustable font size, line spacing, text width and light, sepia or dark pages.

Read aloud

Let the book read itself to you with your device's text-to-speech voice, also with the screen locked, with a sleep timer and controls on the lock screen.

Bookmarks, highlights and notes

Bookmark any place, highlight paragraphs in five colours, add notes, and find them all again in the side panel.

Search inside books

Find any word or phrase in the book you are reading, with every match marked in the text.

Reading log

Keep track of the books you have read and want to read, see your reading time and statistics, set a daily reading goal, and export your log in the Goodreads format.

Find, sort and filter

Accent-insensitive search, sorting by title, author, series, rating or date, and filters by author, series, tag, publisher, language, format and rating. Reading progress shows on every cover.

Tidy library

Edit book details and covers, change many books at once, find duplicates, spot invalid ISBNs, and pick languages from a full list.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. General provisions

1.1 The Calibre Touch application is developed by Richard Kurjak.

1.2 The application does not require the user to provide any personal or confidential information, needs no account and does not collect personal data during its use.

1.3 The application itself does not send any data over the internet. It does not show advertisements and does not send usage statistics, anonymous or otherwise. Links to this website open in your web browser.

2. Permissions and what they are used for

2.1 Access to files. The application needs access to the folder you choose for your library. It uses it to list, search and open your books, and to manage your library at your request: it adds books to the library folder, renames and moves a book's files inside the library folder when you change its title or author, deletes books when you ask it to, and writes the library's database (metadata.db), each book's metadata.opf and cover.jpg. It does not change files outside the library folder, except files you explicitly choose to save (see 3.3).

2.2 Camera. Used only when you take a photo for a book cover. The photo is used as the book's cover in your library and is not sent anywhere.

2.3 Notifications and background playback. Used only for reading aloud: it lets reading continue with the screen locked and shows play, pause and stop controls in the notification area and on the lock screen.

3. Data stored on your device

3.1 Your reading log, reading time, "want to read" list, bookmarks, highlights, notes and reading progress are stored in the application's own private storage on your device, never in your library folder. They are deleted when the application is uninstalled.

3.2 Reading positions and settings are also stored only on your device.

3.3 A backup of your reading data, or an export of your reading log (CSV), is written only to the place you choose, and only when you ask for it.

4. Reading aloud

Reading aloud uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. The text being read is passed to that engine. Depending on the engine and voice you have chosen in your device's settings, the engine may process text online, under its own privacy policy.

5. Providing information to third parties

The application does not transfer any information to third parties. When you choose to open a book in another app, that app receives the book file.
