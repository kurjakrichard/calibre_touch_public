E-book library for touchscreens

We love reading books

Calibre Touch is a library reader and standalone e-book manager designed for touchscreen devices. Browse, search and open your books and documents with a simple, touch-friendly interface.

See features Privacy policy

What it does

Works with Calibre libraries

Calibre Touch can open and use existing Calibre libraries. However, this compatibility comes with absolutely no guarantee: Calibre Touch is an independent app, not affiliated with Calibre. If you use both apps on the same library, you do so at your own risk. Please keep a backup of your library.

Built for touch

Designed for every touchscreen device: phones, tablets and touchscreen laptops. Large targets, swipeable lists and a layout that adapts to any screen.

Standalone library

Works as its own e-book library manager, with no desktop Calibre installation required.

Fast search

Find books and documents in your lists quickly, with accent-insensitive search.

Opens your files

Open books and documents straight from your library in the reader app of your choice.

Privacy policy

1. General provisions

1.1 The Calibre Touch application is developed by Richard Kurjak.

1.2 The application does not require the user to provide any personal or confidential information and does not collect it during the use of the application.

1.3 To open books and documents, display them in the application lists, and search them, the system permission to access files is required. The application does not modify, move, or forward files.

1.4 The application does not send anonymous application usage statistics.

The application does not transfer the received information to third parties.
