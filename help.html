Getting started

The first time you open Calibre Touch, a short setup guides you through choosing where your books are kept.

1

Start the app

Calibre Touch opens with its splash screen while it loads.

2

Welcome

The welcome screen introduces the app. Tap Next to continue. You can also tap the dots at the bottom to move between setup pages.

3

Choose where your books are kept

Calibre Touch shows the folder it will use for your library.

  • Use default keeps your books in the app's own folder.
  • Choose folder lets you pick another folder, for example an existing Calibre library on your device.

Don't put the library on a network drive or in a cloud-synced folder (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox…): that is not safe and can corrupt it.

4

Allow file access

If you choose your own folder, Android asks for file access. Switch on Allow access to manage all files, then go back to the app with the back arrow.

Calibre Touch only uses this permission to list, search and open your books. See the privacy policy.

5

Pick the folder

Browse to the folder that holds your books and tap Use this folder. When Android asks whether to allow access to the folder, tap Allow.

6

You're all set

Tap Get Started to open your library. You can change these choices later in Settings.

7

Add your books

A new library is empty. Tap + in the bottom-right corner to add a book file (EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, PDF, DOCX, ODT, RTF, TXT and more). You can also open a book file with Calibre Touch from another app, or share it to Calibre Touch.

Menu and settings

The side menu

Tap the ☰ menu button in the top-left corner to open the side menu.

  • Continue reading opens the book you read last. Press and hold it to choose from the books you read recently.
  • Reading log opens your reading log and statistics (when the reading log is on).
  • Duplicates lists books that are probably in your library more than once.
  • Settings opens the app settings.
  • Help opens this page.

Settings

  • Language: the language of the app.
  • Where should Calibre Touch keep your books? Change your library folder with Choose folder, or go back to the app's own folder with Use default.
  • Dark theme: switch between light and dark appearance.
  • Built-in reader: open books inside Calibre Touch. Supported formats: EPUB, MOBI/AZW3, FB2, TXT, DOCX, ODT, RTF and PDF.
  • Open last book on startup: continue reading where you left off when the app starts.
  • Reading log: measure your reading time and keep a log of the books you read. When it is off, nothing is measured and the reading log is hidden.
  • Reading data: Back up saves your reading log, statistics, "want to read" list, bookmarks and highlights into one file, wherever you choose. Restore puts such a backup back; your current reading data is replaced.

Tap Done to save your changes.

Searching and filtering

Find a book

Tap the search icon at the top of the screen, type words from the title, author, series, tags or description, then press Enter or tap the search icon in the field. Accents don't matter, so "tuz" also finds "tűz".

Tap ✕ to clear the search and show all your books again.

Filter your library

Tap the filter icon at the top of the screen (the badge shows how many values are picked) and choose a category: author, series, tags, publisher, languages, format or rating. Tick the values you want; long lists have a search field.

  • Inside one category any ticked value matches; across categories every category must match.
  • The picked values appear above your books with the number of books shown. Tap ✕ on a value to remove it, or Clear filters to remove them all.
  • The filter works on the search result too, and lasts until you close the app or switch libraries.

Sort your library

Tap the ⋮ menu, then Sort by, and choose date added, title, author, series, rating, published or last modified, ascending or descending. The order is used in every view and is remembered. Title and author follow Calibre's sort order (for example "The Hobbit" sorts under H).

Library views

Choose how your library is shown: as covers, as a list, or as a table.

Change the view

Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner, then Change view, and choose Covers, List or Table. The current view has a check mark.

A thin bar on a cover (and a percentage in the list and table) shows how far you have read the book; a green check mark shows a book you have logged as finished.

Reading

Tap Read on a book to open it in the built-in reader. Tap the middle of the page to show or hide the bars.

A book's details

Tap a book to see its details: series, publisher, publication date, languages, formats, identifiers, rating, page count and description. Tap a format to open the book in that format.

  • Read opens the book (in the built-in reader, or in the app of your choice if the built-in reader is off).
  • Edit book: see Editing books.
  • Open folder shows the book's files.
  • Add to reading log and Want to read: see Reading log.

Turning pages

Scroll the text, or swipe left and right to turn a page. On a computer use the arrow keys, Page Up / Page Down or Space. Pull past the end of a chapter to go on to the next one. The slider at the bottom moves between chapters and shows the page number and percentage.

The top bar

  • Contents: the side panel with the table of contents, your bookmarks and your highlights.
  • Night mode: switches between light and dark pages.
  • Read aloud: see Reading aloud.
  • ⋮: the reader's menu.

The reader's menu

Tap ⋮ for Search in book (Ctrl+F), Bookmark (Ctrl+B), the Sleep timer and the Reading settings.

Reading settings

In ⋮ → Reading settings you can set the font size, the text width (narrow, medium or full), line spacing, justified text, the reading-aloud speed and the page colour (automatic, light, sepia or dark).

Bookmarks, highlights and notes

  • ⋮ → Bookmark (Ctrl+B) bookmarks the paragraph at the top of the screen. A red ribbon shows that this place is bookmarked.
  • Press and hold a paragraph (right-click on a computer) for its menu: highlight it in one of five colours, add a note, bookmark it, copy it, or read aloud from there.
  • The side panel lists your bookmarks and highlights with their chapter and page. Tap one to jump there; use its ⋮ to edit the note or delete it.

Searching in the book

⋮ → Search in book (Ctrl+F) opens the search panel. Type a word or phrase and press Enter. Every match is listed with its chapter and page, and marked in the text. The bar at the top steps through the matches (F3 / Shift+F3 on a computer); tap ✕ to end the search. Tick Ignore accents to find "kor" in "kör" and "kór" too.

PDF books

PDF books open in a page viewer. Zoom with two fingers or double-tap. Bookmarks work per page and can have a note; searching marks the matches on the pages.

Reading aloud

Listen to a book

Tap the read aloud icon in the top bar. Reading starts at the top of the screen, follows the text and marks the paragraph being read. To start somewhere else, press and hold a paragraph and choose Read aloud from here. The voice and its language come from your device's text-to-speech settings.

On Android, reading goes on with the screen locked or in another app. The notification and the lock screen show the book with buttons for the previous and next paragraph, pause and stop.

Sleep timer

⋮ → Sleep timer stops reading after 5 to 60 minutes, or at the end of the chapter. The minutes left show on the read-aloud icon.

Reading log

The reading log keeps the books you have read and want to read, and measures how much you read. It is stored only on your device and can be turned off in Settings.

Logging books

  • When you reach the end of a book, Calibre Touch offers to log it as read.
  • You can also log a book from its details page or menu, or add one by hand with + in the reading log.
  • Want to read on a book's details page puts it on your "want to read" list.

Statistics and daily goal

The Statistics tab shows the books, pages and reading time of each year, books per month and year, the last 30 days, your reading streak and your most read authors.

Set a daily reading goal at the top of the tab. The circle shows today's minutes, the dots show the last seven days, and the reader tells you when you reach the goal.

Export and import

Export your log as a Goodreads CSV file, which Goodreads, StoryGraph, Bookstry and others can import, and import such files back. Books are matched with your library by ISBN or by title and author.

Editing books

Tap Edit book on a book's details page. Changes are saved with Save, into the Calibre library and the book's metadata.opf.

Details

  • Title, author, publisher, series and number, tags, publication date, identifiers and description.
  • Rating: tap the left half of a star for half a star, the right half for a whole one; tap the current rating to clear it.
  • Languages: tap Add language and search by name or code.
  • An ISBN with a wrong check digit is marked as invalid, here and on the details page.

Cover

Take the cover from a picture file, a photo (on phones) or the book file itself, crop it, resize it, generate a simple cover from the title and author, or remove it. The size of the cover is shown under its title.

Bulk edit and duplicates

Selecting several books

Press and hold a book and choose Select (or Ctrl+click on a computer). While books are selected, tapping a book selects or deselects it. The top bar shows how many are selected, and lets you select all books shown or end the selection.

Editing them together

Tap Edit together to add or remove tags, set or remove the series (optionally numbering the books in the order shown), set or remove the publisher, set the languages or change the rating. Fields left empty change nothing. Title and author are changed book by book.

Finding duplicates

Duplicates in the side menu lists books that are probably the same: the same ISBN (when the books also share an author or title words), the same title and author, or a one-volume edition next to its separate volumes. Different volumes, series and languages are not counted as duplicates.

Open or delete each book from its ⋮ menu, or mark a group as Not duplicates to hide it.
